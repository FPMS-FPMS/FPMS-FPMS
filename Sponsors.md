# Sponsors & Supporters

Organizations that have helped FPMS move from a student project to **WRO Canada National Champions** and onward to the **World Robot Olympiad International Final**.

## Orange Pi

> **“FPMS and Team Canada are proudly supported by Orange Pi, whose high-performance computing platforms help power our autonomous wildfire robotics system.”**

**Orange Pi / Shenzhen Xunlong Software Co., Limited** is an official sponsor of FPMS for our WRO International journey.

Orange Pi is supporting FPMS with:

| Sponsored item | Configuration / quantity |
|---|---|
| Orange Pi 5B | 16GB RAM + 256GB eMMC |
| Orange Pi 5 MAX | 16GB RAM |
| Camera modules | 4 |
| Additional eMMC module | 256GB |
| Orange Pi 5B power supply | 5V / 4A |
| Orange Pi 5 MAX power supply | 5V / 5A |

The Orange Pi platforms support our work in **ROS 2, autonomous navigation, LiDAR processing, computer vision, sensor processing, mission logic, rover development, and aerial robotics**.

### International acknowledgement

At the WRO International Final, FPMS will use the following Orange Pi-approved acknowledgement:

> **“We would like to thank Orange Pi for supporting FPMS and Team Canada on our journey to the World Robot Olympiad International Final. Orange Pi technology plays an important role in the computing and autonomous robotics behind our system.”**

FPMS will display Orange Pi branding at the international booth and provide high-quality photos and videos of the boards, robots, aerial platform, booth, and competition for Orange Pi's promotional use.

🔗 **Official website:** https://www.orangepi.org/

RobotShop has supported FPMS with an official letter of recommendation from **Julie Gendron, Chief Operating Officer**, recognizing the purpose and engineering behind the project.

> *“What impresses us most is the purpose behind their engineering... a thoughtful, socially conscious application of robotics that goes well beyond what is typically expected of students their age.”*

We are grateful to RobotShop for supporting our journey.
